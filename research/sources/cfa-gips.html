GIPS® Standards for Firms

Firms comply with the GIPS® standards to fulfill their ethical duty to fully disclose and fairly present performance, advance fair competitive practices within the industry, and respond to the demands of prospective clients and investors.

Guidance Statements

Guidance Statements include authoritative guidance on a particular topic and may contain additional topic-specific requirements and recommendations. Firms must comply with all Guidance Statements.

Content from prior Guidance Statements was included in the GIPS Standards Handbooks as much as possible. See the Archive webpage for prior Guidance Statements that have been archived.

See the Work in Process webpage for Guidance Statements that have been issued for public comments but have not been finalized.
